# Research baseline — September 2026

Current strategic findings:

1. Position as a human-led applied-AI personal brand, not an automated AI-news account.

2. Spanish-primary is the current launch hypothesis, with English expansion via subtitles, dubbing, X and later long-form.

3. One account first. Do not split English/Spanish into two separate brands until evidence requires it.

4. Real-source visuals should be the default:
- own screenshots
- official launch media
- real product UI
- properly licensed photography
- charts/source posts
AI imagery is secondary.

5. Codex should build and maintain the system, but not serve as the canonical workflow scheduler.

6. A code-first workflow runtime such as Trigger.dev is a strong candidate for cron jobs, queues, retries and human approval waits.

7. Supabase is a strong candidate for structured content data.

8. Google Drive is a strong candidate for large video/media storage.

9. Programmatic graphic generation is preferable to AI-image-generated text-heavy cards.

10. Local transcription on Apple Silicon is viable.

11. Programmatic video generation/editing such as Remotion is worth evaluating because it can be maintained by Codex.

12. Use official APIs/OAuth or reputable schedulers for publishing. Do not try to evade platform bot detection.

13. Postiz is worth evaluating as a publishing abstraction before building every platform integration manually.

14. Start an owned audience early through a newsletter. beehiiv is one candidate.

15. Do not launch Skool/community until repeated audience demand exists.

16. Competitor analysis should focus on outliers relative to each creator’s own baseline, not absolute views.

17. Research architecture should prioritize:
primary sources
→ quality journalism
→ community/discovery
→ creator signals

18. Initial cadence hypothesis:
- 2 face-led short videos/week
- 2–4 editorial posts/week
- 4–6 X posts/replies/week
- 1 newsletter/week

19. The initial product must prove that its research feed is more useful than manually browsing AI news before automating the rest.
